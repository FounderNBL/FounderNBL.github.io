Why we exist

The mission

Operation Build-A-Block is a New Beansland community initiative originated by Freddie Pickens. Our mission is to bring neighbors and families together to improve their communities through neighborhood beautification, youth opportunities, support for elders, and people helping one another. We start with one block, demonstrate what working together can accomplish, and build from there.

The long-term vision is a network of stronger neighborhood relationships, constructive opportunities for young people and repeatable community improvement projects. This is a real on-the-ground initiative, not a fictional NBL World activity.

Proposed first pilot

One block. One real project. Real results.

Our first pilot is a small, organized neighborhood cleanup or beautification effort. The specific block, date, residents, equipment and permissions are still being selected. We will not claim work has happened before it does.

01 · Listen and selectFreddie meets neighbors and identifies a block where people welcome the work.

Common questions

Learn before you join

Is Operation Build-A-Block happening now?

The program is in planning. The first location and event date have not been announced. We are inviting interest and conversations while the first project is organized.

Who can get involved?

Neighbors, adult volunteers, families, local businesses and community organizations may contact New Beansland. Youth participation will require suitable supervision and parent or guardian permission when applicable.

Can I suggest a block or community need?

Yes. Choose “Suggest a neighborhood project” in the interest form and describe the location generally. Please do not include somebody else's private address or personal information.

Is this a paid job or employment program?

No job openings, wages or stipends are currently being offered through this page. Opportunities may be developed later with appropriate funding and legal arrangements.

Can my business sponsor or support the program?

Yes, you can request a discussion. Any sponsorship or funding arrangement must be reviewed and agreed in writing before money moves. This page is not a charitable donation checkout and does not offer tax-deductible receipts.

Will I be signed up when I press the interest button?

The button opens a prepared email in your mail application. You must review and send it yourself. The website does not automatically save your information or enroll you in a database.
