Operation Build-A-Block

Freddie Pickens brought the vision for Operation Build-A-Block to New Beansland: neighbors working together, starting with one block, to make practical improvements in the places they call home.

His focus is on bringing residents, volunteers, local supporters and families into the same conversation, building shared responsibility instead of a traditional boss-and-employees structure.

The vision

Start With a Block. Build a Community.

Operation Build-A-Block is a real neighborhood initiative developed through New Beansland. Its first pilot remains in planning, with the specific neighborhood, date and project to be confirmed alongside residents and collaborators.

Freddie originated the initiative and brings the neighborhood perspective; New Beansland contributes a platform for communication, outreach, project documentation and creative support.

This work is intended to be built with the community, not imposed on it.

Community priorities

Where the work starts

  • Cleaner, better-cared-for neighborhood spaces
  • Constructive opportunities for young people
  • Respectful, consent-based support for elders
  • Neighbors and local businesses collaborating

Documented community background

A public record of community involvement

A 2026 California corporate filing for Opening Minds and Hearts Inc. (OMAH) lists Freddie Pickens of Brooklyn among the organization's named principals, with the filing designation “Other”. The record identifies an organizational connection, not an executive title or a full account of his individual work.

OMAH describes its work as bringing people together for inquiry, dialogue and community connection. Its public description of New York activities includes gatherings and community-facing collaborations in Brooklyn.

Independent organizations: This background is included to document Freddie's community involvement. OMAH is not being presented as an NBL program, sponsor, or partner. Operation Build-A-Block is an NBL Communities initiative, and NBL is not claiming OMAH's projects or nonprofit status.
