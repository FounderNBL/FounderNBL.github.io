How NBL Pulse works

Your guide to posting, friends, livestreams, privacy, pricing and optional social-account connections. This page explains what residents can use now and what New Beansland is still developing.

Last reviewed November 18, 2026 · For the current enabled features, the signed-in Pulse app and its server controls are authoritative.

What you can do on Pulse

  • Create Beats: share text, supported images, one supported short uploaded video, or links to eligible public content.
  • Discover and connect: see available public feeds, search residents, follow people and send friend requests.
  • Interact: react to posts and use comments where your account and the feed permit them. Report or block abuse using the available controls.
  • Message privately: open NBL Messenger, a separate NBL service using the same account. A public Pulse comment is not a private message.
  • Go Live: eligible signed-in residents can start a limited Pulse video broadcast when the service is enabled. See pilot limits below.

The signed-in interface may restrict some media features while safety reviews are applied. Stories, external automatic publishing, external livestream destinations, and saved livestream recordings are not advertised as active Pulse features.

What does Pulse cost?

Account and normal posting
There is currently no separate Pulse purchase price displayed for creating an NBL account or ordinary Pulse posts. Eligible free access remains subject to account and safety rules.
Pulse Live pilot
One free broadcast per resident per calendar month, up to 5 minutes and up to 100 viewers per session. Global pilot limit: 30 starts per month. An individual start may be denied when the limits are reached. Founder operations have separate daily limits.
Beans
Beans memberships, any paid AI allowance, and optional future My Pulse assistance are separate from the current Pulse social-feed pilot. See the published NBL pricing page for current purchasable Beans offers.
The Bag
Prepaid Beanies/Racks purchases and spending are not active. No resident needs to buy Bag credits to use the free Pulse pilot.

Limits are safeguards, not guaranteed uptime or a promise that unlimited free livestreams will be provided. Only purchase an item when an active checkout clearly discloses its price.

Your privacy, safety and choices

  • Audience: Pulse posts submitted as public content may be viewed, shared or saved by others. Check what you are sharing before posting.
  • Account information: your NBL sign-in, profile, posts, comments and relevant activity are processed to operate the network. NBL uses service providers such as Clerk, Stream and Supabase as described in the full privacy policy.
  • Optional analytics: Pulse offers an opt-in Google usage-statistics setting. Your likes and comments do not require you to opt in to that setting.
  • Moderation: reports, abuse prevention and provider moderation may limit or hold content. Do not assume every post or video is automatically approved or that reporting means immediate removal.
  • Age: the public Pulse social experience is intended for residents 13 and older under New Beansland's current terms; it is not the NBL Kidz educational experience.
  • Your data: request access, correction, or deletion through your account or the public deletion instructions. Deleting an NBL post cannot guarantee the deletion of copies another person already shared.

Official legal documents: Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · Account and Data Deletion. Those documents govern; this guide explains the user experience in simpler language.

Facebook and Instagram: what is connected?

Available now: residents can copy or share a link to an eligible public Pulse post using their own device or Facebook's share interface. You choose whether to complete the post on the external app.

Not yet available: NBL does not currently offer a verified one-tap Graph API publisher from Pulse to your Facebook Page or Instagram account. Facebook Login for an NBL account does not grant posting privileges. Messenger connectivity is a separate permission from publishing posts.

Pilot direction: first verify owner-approved publishing to one eligible Facebook Page; then consider an eligible Instagram Professional account. Each account must be explicitly authorized. NBL will never publish on someone else's behalf without clear review and confirmation.

Not in this pilot: personal Facebook timeline auto-posting, Instagram consumer account API publishing, Facebook Live or Instagram Live cross-streaming.

Beans → My Pulse

Planned integration, not yet an active Beans tool.

The goal is for Beans' My Pulse feature to help a signed-in resident understand Pulse, plan content, draft captions and, where authorized tools are available, help manage their own Pulse account.

Beans must explain the current costs and availability, respect the resident's account, and require permission before publishing or changing anything. Your private Beans conversations do not become public Pulse posts by default.

After this is verified on NBL Pulse, additional external social networks can be considered separately with their own permissions.

Need help or want your information removed?

Start in NBL Pulse for posting and feeds, NBL Messenger for private messages, or My Account for access and account settings. For privacy and data deletion, see the deletion instructions or email founder@newbeansland.org.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, private Stream credentials, or Meta access tokens in posts or support requests.
