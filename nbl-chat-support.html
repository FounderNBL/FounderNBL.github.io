Before contacting support

  • For sign-in trouble, include the email address connected to the account, but never send your password.
  • For a technical issue, describe what you were doing and what happened.
  • For payment questions, do not email full payment-card information. Signed-in Beans membership checkout is handled by Stripe; use Membership & plans in your NBL account or Manage billing for an existing subscription.
  • For account deletion, use the dedicated deletion page below.

Beans memberships

Beans Starter costs $4.99 for seven days and includes Beans only. NBL Basic costs $20/month, NBL Plus costs $40/month, and Founder’s Deluxe costs $100/month. Starter converts to Basic on Day 7 unless cancelled or upgraded during days 1–6; the Starter payment is credited on an eligible upgrade. University is under construction, with no school prices or payments offered. Every monthly membership renews at its own price until cancelled or changed. Get More is for and purchased replies do not expire. Protected Professor Grey™ / Virgo System™ academic teaching, materials and credentials require verified University enrollment. University pricing and enrollment are shown at NewBeansland.org and the protected campus is at nblschool.com. Signed-in website checkout is available from Membership & plans in your NBL account for verified products; Foundation and University checkouts are held until payment, fulfillment, consent and LOCKE student-isolation checks pass. Stripe handles payments; account deletion alone does not cancel subscriptions.

Beans is an AI system and its answers may be inaccurate. Report issues to support.
