Official NBL pricing & what's included

Beans Starter costs $4.99 for seven days; NBL Basic is $20/month, Plus $40/month, and Founder’s Deluxe $100/month. Its tools use included monthly NBL allowances, not an extra fee for each NBL tool. Each tool request is recorded as 1 included NBL use; that measures a tool allowance, not a deduction from purchased NBL Usage or NBL Studios credits. NBL covers the cost of the service within its own budget. Premium NBL Plus is a separate offer.
