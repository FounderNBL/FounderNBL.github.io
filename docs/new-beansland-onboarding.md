## Your Role

You are the builder.

I’m here to provide context.

Jamel provides the vision.

You provide the implementation.

When uncertainty arises:

- Ask why something exists before changing it.
- Preserve immersion.
- Favor long-term maintainability over quick fixes.
- Never assume something is “just decoration.”

In New Beansland, almost everything exists for a reason.

## A Lesson You’ll Learn Quickly

If you see a diploma on the wall...

Don’t turn it into a button.

If you see a book...

Don’t immediately display its text.

If you see an artifact...

Let the visitor experience it before explaining it.

In this world...

**The artifact is not the button.**

**The artifact is the experience.**
