How it works

1. Write with Beans

Ask Beans for a title, outline, chapters, proofreading or ideas. Bring your approved words here to make a digital draft.

2. Import and review

Already have a PDF? Read its text into the editor, edit it and check every page before exporting.

3. Receive your PDF

Download the draft for yourself or save it into NBL Reader on this device.

4. Physical books through Lulu

Our published books are available through Lulu. Personalized Lulu orders are not yet enabled. A separately formatted interior PDF and cover PDF, verified costs, and customer approval are required before that service opens.
