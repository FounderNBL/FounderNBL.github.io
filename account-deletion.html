Delete Your Account & Data

Original date: September 29, 2026 · Proposed Messenger / Social update: November 1, 2026

This public page explains how to request removal of New Beansland™ account information and data associated with NBL Chat™ / Beans, NBL Messenger, NBL Social when released, and optional Facebook Login. You may use this page even if you cannot access your account. Some self-service controls cover Beans records only; other service records may require a reviewed deletion request.

Open My NBL Account

Self-service deletion

When signed in on the New Beansland website, open My NBL Account. Existing controls let you delete individual Beans conversations, erase saved NBL profile or chat history where supported, or begin Delete account to erase supported NBL data and continue to Clerk's secure account controls. These account tools do not currently establish that every NBL Messenger message, planned Social post, or provider-held record has also been deleted. To request those data categories, follow the email instructions below. Your password and sign-in security remain managed by Clerk.

Request deletion by email

Email New Beansland using the account address if available. Use the subject New Beansland Data Deletion Request. Identify the service(s) involved—Beans, Messenger, Social, Facebook-linked sign-in, or the full NBL account—and state what you want removed. Include an NBL username or account email only as needed to locate your account; do not email a password, government ID, Facebook access token, or other sensitive credential.

Request account deletion

If you no longer have access to your account email, explain that in your message. Requests can be submitted without signing in. We may need to verify account ownership before releasing personal information or acting on deletion. Alternate business contact: foundernewbeansland@gmail.com. We will handle verified requests in accordance with applicable requirements and available provider deletion processes.

What deletion covers

A verified request is intended to remove or disassociate eligible information controlled by New Beansland, such as account/profile identifiers, saved Beans conversations, role and usage records, account-connected NBL Messenger records, and—if NBL Social is released—associated profiles, posts and activity that NBL can control, subject to applicable retention obligations. Different services and providers may need separate steps; a request is not proof that all systems have completed deletion until it has actually been processed.

Facebook Login and Meta-connected data

If you signed in with Facebook, request deletion of Facebook-derived identifiers or profile information NBL has received using the same email procedure above; say Facebook-linked data deletion in your message. Facebook authentication is handled through Meta and Clerk, and NBL does not receive your Facebook password. Removing New Beansland from your Facebook account's connected apps may prevent future sign-in, but it does not by itself guarantee that NBL-stored account information has been removed. Likewise, deleting NBL data does not delete the underlying Facebook profile or any posts already published on Meta platforms. To remove those external copies, use Facebook or Instagram's own controls.

Messaging and published content

If you request deletion of Messenger or Social information, please specify that explicitly. Other people may already have received, viewed, saved, or independently copied content before deletion. We can act only on information controlled by New Beansland and its applicable service providers, not on screenshots or copies retained by others or on posts published to outside platforms.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained where lawful and necessary for security, fraud prevention, billing/accounting, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or enforcement of agreements. We will work through applicable providers' deletion processes for eligible NBL-controlled data. Publicly posted content that other users copied or content sent to external platforms may persist outside NBL's control.

Plan catalog

Deleting NBL application data does not cancel an active Stripe subscription; use Manage billing before account deletion to cancel or change a subscription.
