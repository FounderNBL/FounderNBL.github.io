Verify a Credential.

Enter the NBL Credential Serial Number printed on the completion certificate. NBL serial numbers are created and recorded by New Beansland; they are not barcodes, ISBNs, license numbers, or outside accreditation numbers.

A valid result confirms that the serial matches an official New Beansland credential record.
