New Beansland™ · NBL Services

Privacy Policy

Original effective date: September 30, 2026 · Proposed service disclosures updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains information practices for New Beansland™ accounts, NBL Chat™ and Beans, NBL Messenger, and NBL Social when released, plus separately enrolled University learning. NBL Social feeds are in development and are not yet a public service. Professor Grey™ / Virgo System™ protected course access remains University entitlement-gated.

Information we may process

  • Account information: identifiers such as your email address, authentication account ID, role/access status, and related account metadata when you sign in.
  • Facebook and other optional sign-in: When you choose Facebook Login, Meta and our account provider Clerk may provide approved account information such as an app-specific identity, profile name, email address when available, and the permissions you authorize. We do not ask for your Facebook password. Any age-range information is used only if made available through approved permissions; Facebook sign-in does not conclusively verify age.
  • Messenger and Social activity: For NBL Messenger, this can include your NBL handle, message content and attachments, conversation participants, delivery records, safety reports, blocks, and timestamps. If NBL Social launches, public or audience-selected profiles, posts, photos/videos, comments, reactions, follows, moderation reports, and feed activity may also be processed. Public posts and interactions can be seen or copied by other users; private messages are intended for their recipients and authorized safety or service processes, not the public feed.
  • Chat content: messages you send to Beans or Professor Grey™ and information necessary to generate a response. When you are signed in, conversations may be stored with your NBL account so you can return to them later; signed-out chats are not attached to an NBL account.
  • Usage and service information: session/device identifiers, feature usage, reply counts, membership status, and error/security records. Published offers include Beans monthly; Foundation Materials once, including one month of Beans; Beans + University monthly; qualifying student continuation monthly; and Get More once for . Ongoing memberships renew at their own price until cancelled or changed, with customer consent.
  • Payment information: signed-in website checkout is handed off to Stripe. New Beansland processes account and entitlement identifiers needed to attach a verified purchase to the correct NBL account, but full payment-card details are handled by Stripe. Do not send full card details to New Beansland.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, provide Beans and NBL Chat™, operate NBL Messenger and any released NBL Social features, deliver messages and permitted posts, enforce age/access rules and safety controls, provide protected NBL University processing for separately enrolled verified accounts, retrieve New Beansland information, maintain role and entitlement checks, enforce usage allowances, support users, protect the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and administer account entitlements. Purchased Get More reply balances are described as non-expiring.

Connected Facebook and Instagram features

Facebook Login is optional and uses the existing New Beansland account system. It does not automatically import Facebook posts, photos, friends, likes, or videos. If we later offer an approved Facebook Page or Instagram Professional account connection, we will identify the information and permissions involved and offer posting or sharing controls before publishing content to external accounts. Cross-posted material is also subject to the external platform's rules and data practices. Disconnecting a Meta account is different from asking us to delete information NBL already holds.

AI processing

Beans is an AI system. Chat messages may be transmitted to New Beansland service providers used to generate AI responses. NBL-specific questions may also be matched against New Beansland-controlled knowledge before an AI response is generated. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect; do not rely on them as the sole basis for important legal, medical, financial, safety, or other high-impact decisions.

Service providers

New Beansland services may use third-party providers for functions such as authentication, hosting/database services, communications, social feeds, AI inference, and payments. Current or planned service components may include Clerk for authentication; Meta when you choose Facebook Login or an eligible connected account; Supabase for NBL Messenger, application data, and New Beansland knowledge retrieval; GetStream for NBL Social feeds if released; and OpenAI for AI response generation, transcription, speech, and eligible premium AI tools. The public Chat-plan catalog is not a website purchase flow; any payment provider is relevant only when a supported payment rail is available. Each provider may process information according to its role in delivering the service.

Sharing and sale of personal information

New Beansland does not sell personal information collected through the services described here to advertisers. We may disclose information to service providers that process it on our behalf, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users, New Beansland, or the integrity of the service.

Messages, posts, audience choices, and safety

Messenger conversations are intended for their authorized participants; future Social posts may be visible according to the audience and settings offered for that feature. We may process reports, blocking information, and limited records needed to review abuse, maintain security, or respond to lawful requests. We do not promise that another person will not save or re-share content they can access.

Data retention

Signed-in Beans conversations remain associated with the account until the user deletes a conversation, deletes their saved NBL data, or the records are otherwise removed under New Beansland retention practices. Messenger and, when offered, Social records have their own technical storage and retention processes; deleting Beans history alone does not necessarily delete those records. We retain account, role, usage, support, security, and related records only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service, administer transactions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Some limited records may need to be retained after an account-deletion request for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal, or compliance reasons.

Account and data deletion

You can manage saved NBL data and request account deletion through the New Beansland website account area, or use our public deletion page at newbeansland.org/account-deletion.html. Sign-in and password/security controls are handled by Clerk. Requests to delete Messenger, future Social, and Meta-linked information may require a separate reviewed request where self-service deletion is not yet provided. Account deletion is intended to remove or disassociate eligible NBL-controlled data subject to limited retention required for legitimate security, legal, billing, or compliance reasons. It does not erase a post already published on Facebook or Instagram; you must also manage copies on those services.

Security

We use access controls and server-side role checks designed to protect account and restricted New Beansland information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid sending passwords, full payment credentials, or other highly sensitive information through chat.

Children

NBL Chat™, NBL Messenger, and the planned NBL Social experience are not directed to children under 13. NBL Social is planned as a 13-and-older service. Facebook Login does not itself establish that a user is old enough; age and safety controls remain New Beansland responsibilities. A future NBL Kidz K–12 educational experience will require its own separate child-appropriate access and privacy arrangements before it is offered. If you believe a child has provided information contrary to applicable requirements, contact us.

Your choices

You may stop using the service, sign out, request account or information deletion, and contact us about privacy questions or data requests. Where available, you may also disconnect external accounts or choose not to publish to them. Removing a Facebook connection alone may not remove existing NBL-controlled records. Availability of additional rights may depend on where you live.
