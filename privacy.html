New Beansland™ · NBL Services

Privacy Policy

Original effective date: September 30, 2026 · Updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers New Beansland™ and its services, including NBL Chat™ / Beans, NBL Messenger, NBL Social, connected social-account features, and separately enrolled NBL University. The practices described apply to each feature as it becomes available. Professor Grey™ / Virgo System™ protected course access remains University entitlement-gated.

Information we may process

  • Account and identity information: NBL username/handle, display name, email address, profile details you provide, linked external-account identifiers, account creation and last-login times, authentication account ID, and role/access status when you sign in.
  • Facebook and other optional sign-in: When you choose Facebook Login, Meta and our account provider Clerk may provide approved account information, including a Meta app-scoped user identifier, public profile name, email address where available, and information covered by permissions you authorize. We do not ask for or store your Facebook password. If an approved age-range permission is offered, we may use the age band to determine eligibility, safety protections, or whether additional checks are needed; it is not a guarantee of age verification. We do not automatically read a person's Facebook photos, likes, friends, videos, or timeline simply because they sign in.
  • Messenger and Social activity: For NBL Messenger, this can include your NBL handle, message content and attachments, conversation participants, delivery records, safety reports, blocks, and timestamps. For NBL Social features you use, this also includes profiles, posts, media, captions, comments, likes/reactions, follows, saved content if offered, feeds, moderation reports, and their visibility choices. Public posts and interactions can be seen or copied by other users; private messages are intended for their recipients and authorized safety or service processes, not the public feed.
  • Chat content: messages you send to Beans or Professor Grey™ and information necessary to generate a response. When you are signed in, conversations may be stored with your NBL account so you can return to them later; signed-out chats are not attached to an NBL account.
  • Technical and security records: IP address, browser/device characteristics, operating system, activity timestamps, approximate location inferred from network connections, sign-in/session records, cookie and similar storage identifiers, request logs, and abuse-prevention signals as needed for login, functionality, service reliability, and security. We do not infer precise GPS location unless a specific feature requests permission.
  • Usage and service information: session/device identifiers, feature usage, reply counts, membership status, and error/security records. Published offers include Beans monthly; Foundation Materials once, including one month of Beans; Beans + University monthly; qualifying student continuation monthly; and Get More once for . Ongoing memberships renew at their own price until cancelled or changed, with customer consent.
  • Payment information: signed-in website checkout is handed off to Stripe. New Beansland processes account and entitlement identifiers needed to attach a verified purchase to the correct NBL account, but full payment-card details are handled by Stripe. Do not send full card details to New Beansland.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, provide Beans and NBL Chat™, operate NBL Messenger and any released NBL Social features, deliver messages and permitted posts, enforce age/access rules and safety controls, provide protected NBL University processing for separately enrolled verified accounts, retrieve New Beansland information, maintain role and entitlement checks, enforce usage allowances, support users, protect the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and administer account entitlements. Purchased Get More reply balances are described as non-expiring.

Connected Facebook and Instagram features

Facebook Login is optional and uses the existing New Beansland account system. It does not automatically import Facebook posts, photos, friends, likes, or videos. When supported connections to Facebook Pages or Instagram Professional accounts are available, we request only the approved permissions needed for the selected feature, such as listing eligible connected Pages or publishing selected media. Users control whether to connect and publish to eligible external accounts. Inward syncing of permitted Page/Professional content, if offered, is separate from Facebook Login. Cross-posted material is also subject to the external platform's rules and data practices. Disconnecting a Meta account is different from asking us to delete information NBL already holds.

AI processing

Beans is an AI system. Chat messages may be transmitted to New Beansland service providers used to generate AI responses. NBL-specific questions may also be matched against New Beansland-controlled knowledge before an AI response is generated. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect; do not rely on them as the sole basis for important legal, medical, financial, safety, or other high-impact decisions.

Service providers

New Beansland services may use third-party providers for functions such as authentication, hosting/database services, communications, social feeds, AI inference, and payments. Current or planned service components may include Clerk for authentication; Meta when you choose Facebook Login or an eligible connected account; Supabase for NBL Messenger, application data, and New Beansland knowledge retrieval; GetStream for NBL Social feeds if released; and OpenAI for AI response generation, transcription, speech, and eligible premium AI tools. The public Chat-plan catalog is not a website purchase flow; any payment provider is relevant only when a supported payment rail is available. Each provider may process information according to its role in delivering the service.

Sharing and sale of personal information

New Beansland does not sell personal information collected through the services described here to advertisers. We do not use Facebook Login data to create advertising audiences or market data lists. We may disclose information to service providers that process it on our behalf, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users, New Beansland, or the integrity of the service.

Messages, posts, audience choices, and safety

Messenger conversations are intended for their authorized participants; future Social posts may be visible according to the audience and settings offered for that feature. We may process reports, blocking information, and limited records needed to review abuse, maintain security, or respond to lawful requests. We do not promise that another person will not save or re-share content they can access.

Data retention

Signed-in Beans conversations remain associated with the account until the user deletes a conversation, deletes their saved NBL data, or the records are otherwise removed under New Beansland retention practices. Messenger and Social records have their own technical storage and retention processes; deleting Beans history alone does not necessarily delete those records. We retain data only for the period needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, process abuse reports, secure systems, and resolve disputes. Deletion or de-identification is carried out when required or appropriate, with limited lawful exceptions. Backups and provider systems may require additional processing time. We retain account, role, usage, support, security, and related records only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service, administer transactions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Some limited records may need to be retained after an account-deletion request for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal, or compliance reasons.

Account and data deletion

You can manage saved NBL data and request account deletion through the New Beansland website account area, or use our public deletion page at newbeansland.org/account-deletion.html. Sign-in and password/security controls are handled by Clerk. Requests to delete Messenger, future Social, and Meta-linked information may require a separate reviewed request where self-service deletion is not yet provided. Account deletion is intended to remove or disassociate eligible NBL-controlled data subject to limited retention required for legitimate security, legal, billing, or compliance reasons. It does not erase a post already published on Facebook or Instagram; you must also manage copies on those services.

Cookies and session storage

We use account/session cookies and similar browser storage to maintain sign-in, remember necessary settings, operate communications and feeds, and protect the service. Our providers may set their own operational cookies or tokens under their applicable policies. Where consent is legally required for optional tracking, those features must not be enabled before an appropriate choice is available.

Privacy rights and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, and may exercise other rights available under applicable law. Write to the contact below with the account email or NBL handle and the action requested. We may verify ownership before giving access to or deleting information, and will respond as required by applicable law. You can also use public deletion instructions without signing in. To change who sees Social content, use available audience controls; deleting it from NBL does not erase copies that other people or external services already saved.

Security

We use access controls and server-side role checks designed to protect account, messages, and restricted New Beansland information. Some authorized technical staff or service providers may process private information when needed for delivery, support, safety, legal requirements, or troubleshooting; NBL Messenger should not be assumed to provide end-to-end encryption unless explicitly labeled as such in the product. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid sending passwords, full payment credentials, or other highly sensitive information through chat.

Children

NBL Chat™, NBL Messenger, and the planned NBL Social experience are not directed to children under 13. NBL Social is intended for users age 13 and older. Facebook Login does not itself establish that a user is old enough; age and safety controls remain New Beansland responsibilities. NBL Kidz K–12 education is governed by separate child-appropriate access and privacy terms when offered; 13+ public social sign-in never grants access to younger students' educational accounts. If you believe a child has provided information contrary to applicable requirements, contact us.

Your choices

You may stop using the service, sign out, request account or information deletion, and contact us about privacy questions or data requests. Where available, you may also disconnect external accounts or choose not to publish to them. Removing a Facebook connection alone may not remove existing NBL-controlled records. Availability of additional rights may depend on where you live.
