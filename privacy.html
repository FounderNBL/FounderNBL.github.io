New Beansland™ · NBL Chat™

Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how New Beansland™ handles information for the Beans AI assistant, NBL Chat human messaging, NBL Plus tools, and NBL University. Professor Grey™ and Virgo guided academic processing is restricted to eligible University accounts. The name of the underlying AI provider does not change which NBL product someone is using.

Information we may process

  • Account information: identifiers such as your email address, authentication account ID, role/access status, and related account metadata when you sign in.
  • Chat content: messages you send to Beans or Professor Grey™ and information necessary to generate a response. When you are signed in, conversations may be stored with your NBL account so you can return to them later; signed-out chats are not attached to an NBL account.
  • Usage and service information: session/device identifiers, AI tool/model usage and NBL Usage ledger entries, membership and entitlement status, errors and security events.   • Payment information: signed-in website checkout is handed off to Stripe. New Beansland processes account and entitlement identifiers needed to attach a verified purchase to the correct NBL account, but full payment-card details are handled by Stripe. Do not send full card details to New Beansland.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, deliver Beans AI replies and NBL Chat messages, support approved NBL Plus tools and University coursework, maintain conversation history where enabled, verify LOCKE access and University account ownership, process provider-metered NBL Usage, fulfill purchases where enabled, prevent abuse and respond to requests. Required official University grading is tuition-included and not deducted from the learner's NBL Usage wallet. Purchased and earned NBL Usage balances are described as non-expiring.

AI processing

Beans is an AI system. Chat messages may be transmitted to New Beansland service providers used to generate AI responses. NBL-specific questions may also be matched against New Beansland-controlled knowledge before an AI response is generated. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect; do not rely on them as the sole basis for important legal, medical, financial, safety, or other high-impact decisions.

Service providers

New Beansland may use third-party services for authentication, data hosting, AI inference and billing, including Clerk, Supabase, OpenAI and Stripe where applicable. The public plans page explains customer prices; providers' wholesale operating costs are not billed directly to customers. Website checkout, when enabled, uses Stripe; Google Play digital purchases follow applicable platform billing rules. Providers process data according to their role in operating the service.

Sharing and sale of personal information

New Beansland does not use NBL Chat™ to sell personal information to advertisers. We may disclose information to service providers that process it on our behalf, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users, New Beansland, or the integrity of the service.

Data retention

Signed-in Beans conversations remain associated with the account until the user deletes a conversation, deletes their saved NBL data, or the records are otherwise removed under New Beansland retention practices. We retain account, role, usage, support, security, and related records only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service, administer transactions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Some limited records may need to be retained after an account-deletion request for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal, or compliance reasons.

Account and data deletion

You can manage saved NBL data and request account deletion through the New Beansland website account area, or use our public deletion page at newbeansland.org/account-deletion.html. Sign-in and password/security controls are handled by Clerk. Deleting an account is intended to remove associated NBL Chat™ account data subject to limited retention that may be required for legitimate security, legal, billing, or compliance reasons.

Security

We use access controls and server-side role checks designed to protect account and restricted New Beansland information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid sending passwords, full payment credentials, or other highly sensitive information through chat.

Children

NBL Chat™ is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable requirements, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Your choices

You may stop using the service, sign out, request account deletion, and contact us about privacy questions or data requests. Availability of additional rights may depend on where you live.
