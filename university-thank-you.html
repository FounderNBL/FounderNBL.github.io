Welcome to the Foundation Program.

Stripe has completed the checkout. New Beansland handles the first version of fulfillment personally, so the next steps are intentionally simple.

1. Keep your Stripe receipt. It confirms the payment and the email used for enrollment.
2. Digital books are sent by New Beansland. The Founder will send the Student Course Book and Discernment Workbook digital editions to the enrollment email.

4. Course instructions follow. You can begin with the digital materials without waiting for the physical shipment.
