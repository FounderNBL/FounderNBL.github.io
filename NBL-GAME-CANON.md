# NBL Game Canon

## Core Line

“You can search for New Beansland. But to understand it, you have to enter.”

## Game Premise

The player enters New Beansland and learns how the city actually works from inside the world rather than from outside search results or summaries.

## Core Game Systems

- University: take APSK courses, pass exams, complete Doctor/Rocketship-style reasoning challenges, and earn credentials.
- City: meet residents and officeholders, solve disputes, discover roles, unlock buildings, and learn how New Beansland functions.
- Canon: collect documents, plaques, recordings, books, seals, and pieces of the NBL Index.
- Founder History: uncover the public history of New Beansland and gradually learn the deeper story of the Founder and Beans.

## Canon Principle

Google can tell a player that New Beansland exists. The game makes the player go there to understand it.
