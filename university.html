01 A welcome from the Founder

What are you really here to learn?

Welcome to New Beansland University. I didn't build this place just to hand you another set of answers and tell you that makes you smart.

People will tell you something with all the confidence in the world. That doesn't automatically make it true. They'll wave a title around, show you a piece of paper, make a comparison that sounds good—and sometimes nobody stops to ask whether any of it actually proves the point.

That's where we start. Not with what somebody wants you to believe. With what they're saying, what they mean, where the evidence came from, and whether the conclusion really follows.

You don't have to agree with me to belong here. You do have to be willing to think, be challenged, and change your mind when the facts earn it. That's the Frontier.

— From the Mind of the Founder Founder welcome and introduction to the Frontier.

03 The academic path

First, learn how to examine an idea.

The Foundation is the starting point. You'll work through inquiry, analogies, and evidence—not just memorize somebody's conclusions.

FOUNDATION · APSK 101

Foundations of Skeptical Inquiry

Ask better questions. Recognize what you know, what you assume, and what still needs proof.

FOUNDATION · ANSY 110

Introduction to Analogical Reasoning

Know when two things are meaningfully alike—and exactly where the comparison breaks.

FOUNDATION · EBPR 120

Foundations of Evidence-Based Practice

Trace the evidence, question the source and let the strength of the proof set your confidence.

These are the currently prepared Foundation courses. The wider University and future courses are unfinished; nothing here represents an accreditation claim or an official degree program.
