Jamel Hawkins

Founder of New Beansland

Jamel Hawkins is the founder of New Beansland, a creative home and public archive for books, stories, questions, frameworks, and connected fictional worlds. His work includes The Doctor/Rocketship Test, People Zoo, and the wider New Beansland creative universe.

About Jamel Hawkins

Hawkins develops projects that move between fiction, philosophy, reasoning, worldbuilding, and interactive media. New Beansland serves as the central home for that work and for the growing collection of books, stories, experiments, and creative tools connected to it.

The Founder’s Office on New Beansland is the interactive entry point into his role, projects, history, and the larger world behind the site.
